donate column treatments

The Contact section is a two-column block — the live Discord widget on the left, the donate column on the right (shown ghosted for context in each stage below). PromptPay is the primary channel and should be the focal point; Ko-fi / PayPal are secondary. These four treatments all reuse the existing lab-terminal vocabulary — --color-nixie-base, the stone card gradients, the mono eyebrow chrome — and only differ in how the channels are composed and ranked. QR art here is a stylized placeholder; the real promptpay-qr.png drops straight in.

Option A — Recommended

Featured QR + secondary rail

best when one channel dominates · 2–4 total

PromptPay gets a glowing amber focal panel with the QR front-and-centre; the remaining channels sit in a compact vertical rail beside it. Clearest hierarchy of the four — the eye lands on the QR first, secondary channels stay one tap away. This is the direction currently wired into the build.

Option B

Branded channel tiles

best when channels are equal · 2–4 total

Every destination is its own card in a grid, carrying its real brand colour that ignites on hover (the same brand-ignite trick as the navbar social icons). Most colourful and scannable; treats all channels as peers rather than ranking one. PromptPay keeps a mini-QR thumbnail so it still reads as "scan to pay".

Option C

Terminal funding ledger

best for many channels · most "lab readout"

One terminal panel; each channel is a monospace readout row prefixed with a > prompt. PromptPay expands inline to reveal its QR + ID on click. Leans hardest into the Future Gadget Lab console motif and scales to any number of channels without changing the layout — but the QR is one tap less immediate than A/D.

Option D

Split scan console

boldest · strongest single moment

A two-pane console: a large QR "scan pad" with corner brackets and a sweeping scanline on the left, a channel selector on the right where the active method is highlighted. The most memorable, most on-theme moment — it literally looks like the lab's terminal — at the cost of being the most custom to build and maintain.

Recommendation: Option A (featured QR + rail) is the safest fit for "PromptPay is primary" — clearest hierarchy, least custom code, and it's already wired into the build so locking it is zero extra work. Option D (split scan console) is the boldest brand moment if you want the donate column to be a memorable beat rather than a utility. Option B is the move if the channels are really equal and you want them colour-coded; Option C if you expect to add many more channels over time.
