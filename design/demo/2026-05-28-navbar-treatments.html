navigation treatments

Two ways to make the active route and hover state read clearly on the floating glass navbar — without a pale rectangle background. Both reuse the existing Nixie vocabulary (--color-nixie-base, the bifrost-glow family). Hover any link; the third item is set to the persistent active state in both rows.

01

nixie ignite

The label itself lights up. Amber text-shadow at low intensity warms in on hover, with a 1px underline drawing from the left. Active route is the looping version of the same state — like a Nixie filament holding its glow.

Hover
Label color shifts to --color-nixie-base with a low-intensity text-shadow (~6–14px). Underline draws in left → right over 320ms.
Active
Persistent glow + underline, gently breathing on a 3.2s loop — quieter than the hero bifrost-glow so it doesn't fight for attention.
Tradeoff
Brightest of the two. Looks like the tubes warming up — but watch the glow budget on dense menus.
02

bracket markers

Terminal selection metaphor. Square brackets fade in around the label on hover, slide into final position. Active route holds the brackets and blinks them like a console cursor — almost zero glow budget, reads as system UI.
