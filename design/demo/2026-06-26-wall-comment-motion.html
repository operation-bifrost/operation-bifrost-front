wall comment motion

Chrome is locked to Option C (quote drift) — ghosted quote mark, mono author, no surface, no glow. This pass is about lifecycle. Today the wall computes a fixed grid position per slot and rotateBubble swaps a new comment into the same slot, so a quote fades out and the next fades in at the exact same coordinates. Both previews below run live with a 16-comment pool.

Requirements addressed
✓ A comment never reappears at the spot it just left — recycled text always enters at a fresh position.
✓ No two quotes overlap — lane time-offsets (M1) and pre-spaced free anchors (M2) guarantee separation.
✓ Motion stays on transform / opacity only (compositor-friendly); reduced-motion falls back to a static spaced layout.

Motion 1 — Recommended

Rising stream

matches "ลอยขึ้นบนกำแพง" · continuous · self-spacing

Quotes literally float up the wall. Three lanes each carry two quotes drifting upward at slightly different speeds; a quote enters low, rises, and fades near the top, then recycles to the bottom with the next pooled comment. Because the two quotes in a lane are offset by half a cycle, they stay roughly half-a-travel apart and never collide. The most natural reading of the copy and the calmest to watch — nothing ever pops in place.

— Live · drifting upward
Motion 2

Drift + relocate

closest to current code · gentle constellation

Keeps the scattered "constellation" feel: five quotes rest at well-spaced anchors doing a small bob. Every quote rests for a wide random time (5–13s) then moves on its own — no queue, no fixed cadence, so relocations are sporadic with no detectable order. The only rule is that two quotes won't start fading at the same instant; if that would happen the later one waits a random extra beat and retries. A leaving quote settles in at a different free anchor with a fresh random comment, collision-free.

— Live · sporadic · no fixed order

Recommendation: Motion 1 (rising stream) — it's the literal reading of ข้อความจะลอยขึ้นบนกำแพง, never pops in place, and self-spaces without any collision bookkeeping (the lane offsets do the work). In React it becomes lanes of CSS-animated quotes whose text advances to the next pooled comment on animationiteration (swap happens at opacity 0, so it's invisible). Motion 2 is the safer pick if you want to keep the current scattered layout and minimise the diff.
